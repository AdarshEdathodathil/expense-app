# Trace privacy policy

**Last updated: 22 November 2026**

Trace stores account, household, income, expense, budget, and category data in
Firebase services so household members can see the same ledger on their own
devices. Data is associated with the signed-in account and shared only with
members of the selected household.

Trace may collect diagnostic crash reports, anonymous product usage events, and
device integrity signals through Firebase Crashlytics, Analytics, and App Check.
These services help us keep the app reliable and protect the backend from abuse.

We do not sell personal data. We retain account and household records while the
account remains active, and we remove them when the account or household is
deleted according to the product's retention process.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the app owner through the
support address published with the store listing.
